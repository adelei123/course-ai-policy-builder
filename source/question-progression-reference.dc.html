Course AI Policy Builder — Complete Question Progression

Reviewer reference for TEP and instructional designers · Generated from the current activity · September 2026

About this document

The Course AI Policy Builder is a fourteen-step activity that walks a University of Oregon instructor through a series of course-level decisions about generative AI and assembles those decisions into policy language. Instructors answer one screen at a time and the draft builds as they go; the activity ends with two independent, fully editable outputs — a syllabus-ready summary and the complete Course AI Policy for Canvas.

This document reproduces every screen, question, answer option, and the exact syllabus language each option contributes — in the order an instructor encounters them — so the activity can be reviewed without clicking through it. Conditional questions appear directly beneath the answer that triggers them, with the triggering condition labeled.

Nothing here is paraphrased. All question text, option labels, and generated policy language are reproduced verbatim from the activity’s current source.

Using GenAI during the work process is not the same as including AI-generated content in submitted work. First, decide what may appear in submitted work and when students should tell you that they used GenAI.

Consider what the submitted work is meant to demonstrate. If students include AI-generated content, decide whether they must evaluate, verify, revise, or otherwise transform it—and make those expectations clear in the assignment instructions.

This includes AI-generated text, images, code, audio, video, translations, analyses, or other material incorporated into what a student submits.
